# Product FAQ

## What plans are available?
We offer three plans: Starter (up to 5 users), Growth (up to 25 users), and
Enterprise (unlimited users, custom SLA). All plans include core reporting;
Growth and Enterprise add API access and SSO.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes — every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.
Trial accounts are limited to 3 users regardless of the plan selected.

## How is data backed up?
Customer data is backed up every 6 hours with a 30-day retention window on
Growth and Enterprise plans, and a 7-day window on Starter.

## Can I export my data?
Yes, at any time, in CSV or JSON format, from Settings > Data Export. Exports
on the Starter plan are limited to 10,000 rows per file.
