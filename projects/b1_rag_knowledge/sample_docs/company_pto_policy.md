# PTO & Leave Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off (PTO) per month of employment,
totaling 18 days per year. PTO accrual begins on the employee's start date and is
prorated for the first partial month.

## Requesting Time Off
Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least 5 business days
in advance for requests longer than 2 days. Requests of 1-2 days can be submitted
with 48 hours notice. Manager approval is required before time off is confirmed.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any
remaining unused days beyond that are forfeited on December 31st, except where
prohibited by local law.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not accrued — employees receive 10 sick
days per calendar year, available immediately on their start date.
